MS Teams Observability is a collector + backend integration solution for Ops and IT teams: it pulls Microsoft Teams telemetry from Microsoft Graph and turns it into actionable dashboards and incident context in Dynatrace or Splunk.

## What You Get

- **Faster Incident Triage**: correlate call degradations with Microsoft advisories and isolate impacted users or sites faster.
- **End-to-End Call Quality Visibility**: track call quality from summary to stream-level metrics across users, devices, and networks.
- **Multi-Backend Ready**: collect once and route telemetry to Dynatrace or Splunk based on your operating model.

<Steps>

## How It Works

```mermaid
flowchart LR
    A[Microsoft Graph API] --> B[MS Teams Observability Collector]
    B --> C[Dynatrace Backend]
    B --> D[Splunk Backend]
    C --> E[Dashboards and App Pages]
    D --> F[Dashboards and Searches]
```

One collector ingests Teams telemetry once and forwards it to your operational backend.

1. **Authenticate and collect**: the collector uses your Azure app registration to call Microsoft Graph on a schedule.
2. **Normalize and enrich**: records are transformed into consistent event families and enriched with context used for operations and troubleshooting.
3. **Export to one or more backends**: the same collection cycle can send data to Dynatrace, Splunk, or both.
4. **Operate from dashboards and app pages**: teams investigate degraded calls, impacted users/sites, and Microsoft advisories from a single workflow.

Typical telemetry available after ingestion includes:

- Call metadata and stream quality indicators (for example RTT, jitter, and packet loss).
- PSTN / Direct Routing / Auto Attendant / Call Queue data.
- Microsoft service announcements and collector health signals.
