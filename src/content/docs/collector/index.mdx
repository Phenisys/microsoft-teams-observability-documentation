The **MS Teams Observability Collector** (`ms-teams-agent`) retrieves Microsoft Teams telemetry and sends it to Dynatrace, Splunk, or OpenTelemetry backends.

The collector is available in three deployment variants: **v1 (legacy)**, **v2 (current)**, and a **Dynatrace Extension** that runs inside an ActiveGate.
