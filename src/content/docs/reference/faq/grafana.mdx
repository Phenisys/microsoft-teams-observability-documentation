## Deployment

### Is Grafana supported on-premises and in Grafana Cloud?

Yes. Both Grafana OnPrem and Grafana Cloud are supported. A tenant ID is required for Grafana OnPrem.

See <a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}backends/grafana/app/installation/`}>Grafana App Installation</a> for the Loki datasource setup.

## Security

### Are encrypted credentials supported?

Not yet. Encrypted credentials are on the short-term roadmap.

## Capacity

### What is the proven capacity?

Proven up to 70,000 active users. The upper limit is not yet known.

## Access and Retention

### How are access restrictions and retention handled?

Access restrictions and retention rely on Grafana Loki rights.

<Aside type="note">
The Grafana app reads the logs from the Loki datasource configured in <a href={`${import.meta.env.BASE_URL}backends/grafana/app/provisioning/`}>Provisioning</a>. Permissions on that datasource apply to every user of the app.
</Aside>
