The collector supports exporting to any **OTLP-compatible endpoint** (OpenTelemetry Protocol). This enables integration with platforms such as Grafana, Datadog, Dynatrace OTLP ingestion, Jaeger, or any custom OTLP receiver.

## What Is Exported

All standard MS Teams event families are exported as OTLP log records:

- `MSTeams_CallRecords_CallMetadata`
- `MSTeams_CallRecords_StreamDetails`
- `MSTeams_CallRecords_PSTN`
- `MSTeams_CallRecords_DirectRouting`
- `MSTeams_ServiceAnnouncement`
- `MSTeams_CollectionHealth`

## Use Cases

- Forward to a centralized observability platform alongside other telemetry.
- Integrate with existing OTLP pipelines and collectors (OpenTelemetry Collector, Grafana Agent, etc.).
- Use Dynatrace either through its native backend integration or through the Dynatrace OTLP endpoint when your architecture is OTLP-first.
