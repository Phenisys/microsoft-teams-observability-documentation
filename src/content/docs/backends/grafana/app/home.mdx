The Home page is the landing page of the MS Teams Observability app. It presents a high-level summary of your Teams calling activity over the last 7 days.

## KPI Cards

The top section displays 5 key performance indicators:

| KPI | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Calls** | Number of calls in the time range, with a 7-day sparkline |
| **Good Call Rate** | Percentage of calls classified as Good, with color thresholds (green/red) |
| **Poor Calls** | Count of calls with quality degradation |
| **Unclassified** | Count of calls without sufficient quality data |
| **Unique Callers** | Number of distinct users, with a 7-day sparkline |

## Charts

### Call Health Trend

A stacked bar chart showing daily call volume split by health classification (Good / Poor / Unclassified). Use this to spot quality degradation trends over the selected week.

### Health Distribution

A donut chart showing the proportion of Good, Poor, and Unclassified calls in the current time range.

### Platform Distribution

A pie chart showing call distribution by participant platform (Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, Web). Helps identify which clients are most used in your organization.

## How It Works

The Home page queries the Loki datasource using LogQL queries that aggregate `MSTeams_CallRecords_CallMetadata` and `MSTeams_CallRecords_StreamDetails` logs. All queries are scoped to the 7-day time range.

<Aside type="tip">
For a quick health check, look at the **Good Call Rate** KPI. If it drops below 90%, drill into the Calls or Sites pages to isolate the issue.
</Aside>
