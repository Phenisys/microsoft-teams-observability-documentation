The Home page is the landing page of the MS Teams Observability app. It presents a high-level summary of your Teams calling activity over the selected time range.

## KPIs

The top section displays key performance indicators:

| KPI | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Calls** | Number of calls in the selected time range |
| **Healthy Calls %** | Percentage of calls classified as Good |
| **Active Users** | Number of unique users who made or received calls |
| **Platform Distribution** | Breakdown by platform (Windows, Mac, iOS, Android, Web, Teams Phone) |

## Charts

### Call Health Over Time

A time-series chart showing call volume split by health classification (Good / Poor / Unclassified). Use this to spot quality degradation trends.

### Platform Usage

A pie or bar chart showing the distribution of calls by participant platform. Helps identify which clients are most used in your organization.

### Quality Trends

Aggregated quality metrics over time: average RTT, jitter, and packet loss. Helps detect network degradation before users report it.

## How It Works

The Home page queries the Loki datasource using LogQL queries that aggregate `MSTeams_CallRecords_CallMetadata` and `MSTeams_CallRecords_StreamDetails` logs. All queries are scoped to the selected time range.

<Aside type="tip">
For a quick health check, look at the **Healthy Calls %** KPI. If it drops below 90%, drill into the Calls or Sites pages to isolate the issue.
</Aside>
