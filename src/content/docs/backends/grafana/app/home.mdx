The Home page is the landing tab. It presents a high-level summary of your Teams calling activity.

**Default time range:** Last 7 days (adjustable via the Grafana time picker).

## KPI Cards

Three key performance indicators at the top:

| KPI | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Total Calls** | Number of calls in the selected time range |
| **Healthy Calls %** | Percentage of calls classified as Good |
| **Unique Callers** | Number of distinct users who made or received calls |

## Charts

### Call Health Distribution

A chart showing the proportion of Good, Poor, and Unclassified calls. Use this to spot quality degradation at a glance.

### Platform Distribution

A pie or bar chart showing calls by participant platform (Windows, macOS, iOS, Android, Web).

## How It Works

The Home page queries the Loki datasource using the configured data provider. All metrics are recomputed when the time range changes.

<Aside type="tip">
For a quick health check, look at the **Healthy Calls %** KPI. If it drops significantly, drill into Calls or Sites to isolate the issue.
</Aside>
